10 SERVICES TO CREATE FOR TESTING
==================================

Create these 10 services in Admin → Services. For each one, add the offering name shown below and activate it.


1. Transcript & Marks Card
   Offering: Official Transcript — Standard Processing
   Students request their marksheet or transcript. They upload documents, join a queue, and staff approves after AI checks the files. Use this to test document upload, queue, and staff review.


2. Fee & Finance
   Offering: Duplicate Fee Receipt
   Students apply for a duplicate fee receipt. They must pay online first, then submit the form. Use this to test Razorpay payment before submit and the admin payments page.


3. Career Counselling
   Offering: 1-on-1 Career Counselling Session
   Students book a counselling slot instead of standing in a queue. Meeting can be on Google Meet. Use this to test appointment booking and virtual meeting links.


4. Hostel & Accommodation
   Offering: Hostel Room Allocation — Semester 1
   Only enrolled students can apply for a hostel room. Student can choose queue or appointment. Use this to test eligibility rules and hybrid (queue + appointment) mode.


5. Certificates & Letters
   Offering: Bonafide / Study Certificate
   Simple certificate request — student fills a form only, no file upload needed. Use this to test quick requests and fast queue approval.


6. Identity & ID Cards
   Offering: Student ID Card Reissue
   Student applies for a new ID card and books an appointment. Staff can ask student to upload photo again if it is wrong. Use this to test request correction and re-upload.


7. Scholarships & Financial Aid
   Offering: Merit Scholarship 2026
   Long application with 3 workflow steps. Student pays a fee in the middle of the process. Use this to test multi-step workflow and payment during workflow.


8. Library Services
   Offering: Library Membership Renewal
   Student renews library membership with one document upload. Admin can upload a policy PDF on this service to test AI document extraction. Use this to test knowledge upload and AI offering suggestions.


9. Exam & Revaluation
   Offering: Paper Revaluation — End Semester Exam
   Student pays fee, uploads exam papers, and must confirm one step themselves before staff reviews. Use this to test student workflow step, intake document, and reject application.


10. Internship & Industry Relations
    Offering: Internship NOC Letter — Summer 2026
    Student applies for NOC letter for company internship and books appointment on Tue/Thu only. Staff can approve or reject at each step. Use this to test reject flow, limited appointment days, and 3-step staff approval.
