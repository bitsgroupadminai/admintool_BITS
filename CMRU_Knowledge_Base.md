# CMR University - Complete Knowledge Base

## 1. University Overview
CMR University is a premier, NAAC ‘A’ grade accredited, private university located in Bengaluru, recognised by the University Grants Commission (UGC). It is backed by the CMR Group of Educational Institutions (which includes the NAAC ‘A++’ accredited CMR Institute of Technology).
- **Legacy:** Over 30 years of academic excellence.
- **Community:** Brings together students from more than 40 nationalities across 20+ institutions, spanning K–12 to doctoral studies. Nurtures a thriving community of over 20,000 learners.
- **Vision:** To nurture creative thinkers who will drive positive global change. To provide students with a holistic learning experience to prepare them for successful careers or higher education and equip them to thrive in a VUCA (Volatility, Uncertainty, Complexity, and Ambiguity) world.
- **Accreditation & Recognition:** NAAC 'A' Grade, Recognised by UGC. Secured Gold Grade in the Prestigious QS I-Gauge Indian University Rankings.

## 3. Academic Pedagogy & The CMR Advantage
- **Design Thinking Approach:** Embedded across all programmes. Students learn by doing through user-centric, interdisciplinary, and experiential learning. Develops empathy, creativity, and critical problem-solving skills.
- **Future-Ready Pedagogy:** Blends interdisciplinary learning, Design Thinking, and experiential education. Involves hands-on projects, global exposure, research-led teaching, and personalised mentoring.
- **LEAP (Learning, Engagement & Advancement Programme):** A transformative journey beyond classrooms blending learning, engagement, and advancement through mentorship, leadership, collaboration, and real-world experiences.
- **CCC (Common Core Curriculum):** Anchors every CMR University programme (100% student participation). Builds strong foundations in communication, critical thinking, ethics, sustainability, and leadership.
- **ACCP (Accelerated Credit Course Programme):** Empowers higher secondary and PU students to explore future-ready disciplines online, earning recognised e-certificates and academic credits that carry into CMRU’s undergraduate programmes.
- **Global Immersion Programme (GIP):** Broadens students' education by placing them in overseas academic settings (previously enabled collaborations in Malaysia and South Korea).
- **Personalised Learning Pathways:** Flexible, interdisciplinary curricula with mentoring, certifications, and choice-driven progression.
- **Experiential, Project-Based Learning:** Learning through live projects, simulations, internships, studios, labs, and community engagement.

## 4. Innovation & Infrastructure
- **Maker Space:** A democratic building space for students to bring their ideas to life. Prototype designs and gain exposure to real-world tools through certificate courses.
- **Design Thinking Lab:** An innovative environment to ideate, shape viable ideas, nurture start-ups, and support entrepreneurship.
- **Incubation Cell:** A dynamic platform nurturing entrepreneurial thinking, supporting idea development, and connecting students with industry mentors for real-world validation.
- **Vibrant Campus:** Holistic development and service internship opportunities. Peak Bengaluru Advantage provides unmatched exposure to startups, industry leaders, culture, and urban challenges.

## 5. Placements & Industry Integration
- **Recruiters:** 200+ recruiters including Bosch, Amazon, Flipkart, KPMG, EY, Zomato, Deloitte, Capgemini, IBM, and more.
- **Training & Placements Cell:** Headed by a director and senior professor. Every school has a dedicated placement officer to liaison with corporates.
- **Student Development Programme (SDP):**
  - Structured Career Preparedness Programme (CPP) on Discover-Build-Deliver (DBD) model from the first semester.
  - Industry-specific training and student support mechanisms.
- **Pre-Placement Training Programme:** Soft skills, quantitative aptitude, logical reasoning, and verbal aptitude training.
- **Internships:** Internships with corporates, accompanied by a stipend, are provided to all eligible students as per the programme curriculum.
- **Industry-Integrated Curriculum:** Curricula co-shaped with industry partners for exposure to relevant tools, technologies, and professional expectations.

## 7. Admissions & Entrance Exams
- CMR University honours JEE, COMEDK, KCET, CUET, & other state or National-level entrance exam scores.
- Direct admission is available for CUET students.
- **CET Codes:** B.Tech. (E257), B.Arch. (E245)
- **COMEDK Codes:** B.Tech. (E187), B.Arch. (E187(A))
- **PGCET Codes:** MBA (City Campus - B150, OMBR Campus - B149, Lakeside Campus - B395); MCA (OMBR Campus - C527, Lakeside Campus - C521); M.Tech. (Lakeside Campus - T610)
