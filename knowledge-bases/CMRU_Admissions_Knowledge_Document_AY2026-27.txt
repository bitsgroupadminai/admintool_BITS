G.6 Application Process Steps (Standard Admissions Workflow)
Every programme offering under this admissions service follows the same
application process steps in this order:

Step 1: Document Verification
Handled by: AI + Admissions Verification Team
Purpose: Verify uploaded documents are complete, authentic, and readable.
Outcomes:
- Documents Verified → proceed to Step 2
- Missing / Invalid Documents → return to student for re-upload of mandatory
  documents (Class 10 marksheet, Class 12 marksheet or degree marksheets,
  entrance scorecard if applicable, Government-issued ID proof, Passport-size
  photograph, Signature image)
- Fraud / Serious Mismatch → end workflow rejected (forged marksheet, identity
  mismatch, duplicate application)

Step 2: Eligibility Validation
Handled by: Admissions Officer / AI Rules Engine
Purpose: Verify academic eligibility against programme rules.
Outcomes:
- Eligible → proceed to Step 3
- Eligibility Clarification Required → return to student (foreign board
  equivalency unclear, aggregate calculation dispute, marks conversion required)
- Not Eligible → end workflow rejected (required subjects missing, aggregate
  below threshold, individual subject below minimum)

Step 3: Seat Allocation & Merit Processing
Handled by: Admissions Counsellor / Approver
Purpose: Determine seat availability based on rank, qualifying marks, preferences,
and campus capacity.
Outcomes:
- Seat Available → proceed to Step 4
- Waitlisted → end workflow rejected (waitlist recorded operationally; platform
  terminal state rejected until seat opens)
- No Seat Available → end workflow rejected

Step 4: Offer Release
Handled by: Admissions Approver
Purpose: Generate admission offer for the selected programme and campus.
Outcomes:
- Offer Generated → proceed to Step 5
- Offer Expired → end workflow rejected

Step 5: Fee Payment
Handled by: Student
Purpose: Collect admission / confirmation fee from student.
Outcomes:
- Fee Paid → proceed to Step 6
- Payment Pending → stay in current step; system sends reminders
- Payment Deadline Missed / Offer Lapsed → end workflow rejected

Step 6: Admission Confirmation
Handled by: Admissions Approver
Purpose: Finalize admission after fee confirmation and final checks.
Outcomes:
- Admission Confirmed → end workflow completed
- Final Verification Failure → end workflow rejected
- Document fix needed at final stage → return to student to Step 1

G.7 Fee Payment & Financial Support
- Scholarships worth ₹12 Crores are available.
- Types of Scholarships: CMR Merit Scholarships, CMR Sports Scholarships,
  CMR Defense Scholarships, Entrance Test Rank/Score Based Scholarships,
  CMR Leadership Awards, and more.
- Semester-wise fee payments are supported.
- Education loan assistance is available.
- Accommodation and transport services are available.
- Application processing fee (non-refundable): INR 1,000 for UG programmes;
  INR 1,500 for PG programmes; INR 2,000 for Ph.D. programmes.
- Admission confirmation fee / seat acceptance fee must be paid within 7 calendar
  days of offer release unless the offer letter states a different deadline.
- Fee payment methods: online payment gateway (UPI, net banking, debit/credit card).
- Exact tuition fee amounts vary by programme and campus; students must confirm
  the current fee schedule with admissions@cmr.edu.in or the admissions helpline
  before payment. Do not invent programme-wise tuition figures beyond what is
  stated in an official fee schedule annexure.
