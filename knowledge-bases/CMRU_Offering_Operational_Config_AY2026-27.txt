1.2 COMMON APPLICATION WORKFLOW (6 STEPS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Every programme offering under this admissions service follows these process stages
in this exact order:

Step 1: Document Verification
order: 1
name: Document Verification
description: Verify uploaded documents are complete, authentic, and readable.
handledByType: ai
handledByAssignee: document_verification
slaValue: 24
slaUnit: hours
documentExcerpt: "Step 1: Document Verification — Verify uploaded documents are complete, authentic, and readable."
Outcomes:
- approved (Documents Verified) → next_step order 2
- needs_correction (Missing/Invalid Documents) → return_to_student, requireReupload: Class 10 marksheet, Class 12 marksheet, Entrance exam scorecard, Government-issued ID proof, Passport-size photograph, Signature image
- rejected (Fraud/Serious Mismatch) → end_workflow rejected

Step 2: Eligibility Validation
order: 2
name: Eligibility Validation
description: Verify academic eligibility against programme rules (subjects, aggregate, qualification).
handledByType: ai
handledByAssignee: eligibility_screening
slaValue: 24
slaUnit: hours
documentExcerpt: "Step 2: Eligibility Validation — Verify academic eligibility against programme rules."
Outcomes:
- approved (Eligible) → next_step order 3
- needs_correction (Eligibility Clarification) → return_to_student
- rejected (Not Eligible) → end_workflow rejected

Step 3: Seat Allocation & Merit Processing
order: 3
name: Seat Allocation & Merit Processing
description: Determine seat availability based on rank, qualifying marks, preferences, and campus capacity.
handledByType: staff
handledByAssignee: approver
slaValue: 48
slaUnit: hours
documentExcerpt: "Step 3: Seat Allocation & Merit Processing — Determine seat availability based on rank and preferences."
Outcomes:
- approved (Seat Available) → next_step order 4
- needs_correction (Clarification before seat decision) → return_to_student
- rejected (No Seat Available OR Waitlisted) → end_workflow rejected

Step 4: Offer Release
order: 4
name: Offer Release
description: Generate admission offer for the selected programme and campus.
handledByType: staff
handledByAssignee: approver
slaValue: 24
slaUnit: hours
documentExcerpt: "Step 4: Offer Release — Generate admission offer."
Outcomes:
- approved (Offer Generated) → next_step order 5
- needs_correction → return_to_student
- rejected (Offer Expired) → end_workflow rejected

Step 5: Fee Payment
order: 5
name: Fee Payment

handledByType: student
handledByAssignee: student
slaValue: 7
slaUnit: days
documentExcerpt: "Step 5: Fee Payment — Collect admission confirmation fee from student."
Outcomes:
- approved (Fee Paid) → next_step order 6
- needs_correction (Payment Pending) → return_to_student, returnToStepOrder: 5
- rejected (Payment Deadline Missed / Offer Lapsed) → end_workflow rejected

Step 6: Admission Confirmation

slaValue: 24
